## Quality Notes
- Matplotlib only; seaborn is not used.
- SVG files are the editable vector sources for post-processing in tools such as Inkscape, Illustrator, or Figma.
- PDF files are vector-friendly and intended as the LaTeX-first figure format; PNG files are saved at 300 dpi for preview/compatibility.
- Dataset colors are consistent across figures and use a restrained color-blind-friendly palette.
- Detector family is encoded by marker shape in Fig. 4; dataset is encoded by color.
- High-risk regions and near-F1 callouts are intentionally sparse to preserve IEEE double-column readability.
